New sandbox

A template is a stopped golden sandbox. kaas up <name> --from <template> clones its disk (toolchains, agents, caches) and skips provision/agents. Rule of thumb: what you would put in a Dockerfile goes in a template; what you would put in .env stays in the contract and SecretSpec. Secrets, egress runtime, and auto-stop are never copied.
